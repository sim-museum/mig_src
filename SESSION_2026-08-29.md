## Root-caused, not yet fixed

**PO-78 — CTRL+F6 does not show the bogie view. The key-table loader DROPS ~40 bindings.**

* `KEYBOARD/keys.xml`: `Ctrl+F6` → `OUTREVLOCKTOG` (the outside-reverse-lock toggle).
* `KEYBOARD/keyb3d.bin` entry 84: scancode `0x40`, shiftstate 4, bitflag 224 = `KeyName(112,OUTREVLOCKTOG)*2`. **The binding is in the data.**
* Live table: only 3 of the file's 4 F6 entries loaded. `OUTREVLOCKTOG` appears **zero** times — unreachable by any key.
* Not a blanket failure: `Left Ctrl+F1` loads fine.
* **616 `KeyMapping` entries in the file, 576 live — ~40 dropped.** Ctrl+F6 is only the one the PO pressed.
* Side effect: **S239 was wasted work** — it moved this binding to Ctrl+F6 in `KEYMAPS.H`, but the game loads from the FILE, so the edit could never take effect.
* **Next:** instrument `Reg3dConv` to report every skipped entry and why. Do **not** special-case Ctrl+F6.

**PO-81 — spinning compass.** Hypothesis: `TimedDialSpec::SetTimedAnim` computes `gap = Desired − SrcVal` with no shortest-arc handling, while `acgyrocompass` is a 16-bit field whose −182.04 scaler maps 360° onto the full span — so a wrap crossing sends the card the long way round. Explains the PO's own clue (steady on the ground, spinning airborne). Fix prepared **default-off** (`MA_COMPASS_WRAPFIX=1`), narrow to 16-bit fields because the other timed dials are pressures, not angles. **Unverified:** the A/B ran on a flight that never turned (`hdg` max step 0.0°). `port/compass_wrap.sh` now flies a campaign mission and **refuses to report a verdict unless the heading actually crosses the wrap**.

**PO-82 — white/untextured objects.** Mechanism confirmed by reading: an unresolved texture handle silently becomes `glDisable(GL_TEXTURE_2D)`, so the draw shows its vertex colour — white, or yellow where the vertex colour is. A write-only handle registry (fixed) could hand out freed memory. **Cause still unidentified:** the forced-release experiment destroyed no surfaces, so the graphics-preference path was NOT shown to create dangling handles.
